The full RAGE engine, compiled to WebAssembly and running on WebGPU. Play it right here in your browser — or take it with you.

Play Instantly

Everything You Need To Know

Getting Started

  1. Open the browser build, or download the Grand Theft Auto Windows app and extract the folder.
  2. Wait while the engine streams its assets. The first run pulls the most data; later runs reuse what is already cached on your machine.
  3. At the title screen, choose Story Mode or Sandbox Mode, then pick a map when asked.
  4. Click the game once to capture the mouse and start playing.

Performance Tips

  • Use Chrome or Edge 133 or newer, with hardware acceleration enabled.
  • Close other tabs and heavy apps — the engine needs a large block of memory.
  • On a weaker device, append ?scale=0.75 or ?low=1 to the game URL to render at a lighter setting.
  • The first load streams roughly a gigabyte of world data; after that the data is kept locally and loads far faster.
  • Keep the tab in the foreground while it loads. Background tabs are throttled by the browser.

Frequently Asked

Why is the very first load slow?
The engine streams its assets on demand instead of shipping a multi-gigabyte download. Everything it fetches is cached, so repeat visits are much quicker.
Why does it need Chrome or Edge?
The engine uses WebGPU and multi-threaded WebAssembly with 64-bit memory, which only recent Chromium-based browsers support.
Where are my save games kept?
In your browser's own storage for this site, so they survive a refresh. Clearing site data will remove them.
Does it work on Mac or Linux?
The browser build works anywhere a recent Chrome or Edge with WebGPU is available. The packaged desktop app is Windows only.
